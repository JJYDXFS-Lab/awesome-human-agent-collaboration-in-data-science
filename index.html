Human-Agent Collaboration in Data Science

How do people and AI agents work together on data science and analytical tasks? Explore the papers, research directions, and evidence about where collaboration works and where it breaks down.

Our scope includes complex analytical workflows, related graph analysis and reasoning, and supporting methods and benchmarks. The collection focuses on published research, with selected preprints.

About This Survey

This is an experimental, human-curated and LLM-assisted literature resource. Automated screening helps build the candidate pool; human selection guides deeper reading. Extracted evidence and synthesis support exploration, but have not all been independently verified.

Reading the Analysis

Challenge labels are analytical interpretations, not a settled taxonomy. Yearly evidence counts describe the selected literature, not field-wide prevalence or proof that a problem has been solved.

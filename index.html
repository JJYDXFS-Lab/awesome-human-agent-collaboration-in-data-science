Human-Agent Collaboration in Data Science

A continuously updated collection supporting literature discovery and comparison in human-agent collaboration.

How do people and AI agents work together on data science and analytical tasks?Explore papers and benchmarks, compare selected approaches, and trace reported successes, failures, and limitations back to the original studies.

Our scope includes LLM agents for data analysis, data modeling and machine learning engineering, related graph analysis and reasoning, and supporting methods and benchmarks. The collection focuses on published research, with selected preprints.

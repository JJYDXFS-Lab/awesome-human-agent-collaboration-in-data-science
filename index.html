Human-Agent Collaboration in Data Science

Research focus: human-agent collaboration in data science and complex analytical workflows, with a related line of inquiry into graph analysis, graph reasoning, and their benchmarks. We examine where collaboration breaks down, what existing systems can do, and which problems remain open.

The reviewed literature includes conference papers and manually selected arXiv preprints. The conference coverage table below describes the conference corpus, not the full set of papers selected for deeper analysis.

Research Context

The focus is human-agent interaction in real-world complex tasks, especially data science, analytical workflows, and related benchmarks and methods. The reviewed material spans ML, NLP, and HCI communities.

The paper landscape provides the setting; failure evidence supports more specific questions about planning, verification, coordination, and human judgment.

Interpreting the Results

LLM-assisted extraction and synthesis support ongoing research discussion. Quotes and locations remain available for inspection; challenge labels are analytical interpretations.

Yearly counts describe this selected literature set. They do not establish field-wide prevalence, prove that a problem disappeared, or demonstrate causal transfer between communities.
